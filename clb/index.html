Intergenerational self-help clubs · Adult day care

One senior, two services,
one record.

Sổ CLB số is the club book, digital. One system, five channels: a back office for the operator, an app for staff and volunteers, a docked-tablet station at the meeting table, an app for the family, and an app for the senior themselves. Everything lands on a single record — and it still prints.

Five channels, one data discipline

Consent is a first-class recordFamily pages refuse to render without family-sharing consent; withdrawing it kills links already shared.
Attention levels, never diagnosisNo condition fields, no scored instruments; safety info is written as staff instructions. Medication is a reminder log — taken / declined / none brought.
Each channel sees exactly its shareFamily: no health data. Volunteers: only assigned work. The station resets to idle after 45 seconds. Province: aggregates only.
One door into the dataApps go through the gateway with per-user sessions — access is always decided by the core system; the gateway adds surface, not authority.
